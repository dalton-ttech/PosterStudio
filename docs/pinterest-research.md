# Pinterest Research

Goal: discover useful visual neighborhoods, not literal poster-category clichés.

## Method

Given the brief, produce 5–8 visual directions. For each direction include:
- a short direction name
- why it could fit the brand / occasion
- 5–10 Pinterest search phrases

Prefer adjacent real-world visual domains such as:
- luxury/editorial campaigns
- jewelry/watch photography
- architecture and installation art
- museum/exhibition graphics
- print/material/foil references
- hospitality branding
- annual-report / private-banking art direction
- contemporary oriental art

Avoid relying mainly on literal searches such as `Mid-Autumn poster` or `festival poster`.

The search output is for the human. Do not feed the keyword list into the image-generation prompt.

Only images the user manually downloads enter the library.
